# Metro Bilbao — corporate colours

Compiled 2026-09-21.

Primary source: *Normas de Identidad Corporativa*, Metro Bilbao S.A., vol. 1
(concept: Otl Aicher, Hans Brucklacher, Michael Weiss; design: Weiss Design
Asociados, Getxo). Pages 30 and 32. Recovered from the Wayback Machine copy of
the Issuu upload by Michael Weiss:
`https://web.archive.org/web/2023id_/https://image.isu.pub/101025200600-ee1a1dd7736648af9008f9c87d72b2b3/jpg/page_29.jpg`
(page 30) and `.../page_31.jpg` (page 32). Snapshot 2023-02-26.

All corporate colours are defined in NCS (Natural Color System). The manual
states that Pantone, RAL and 3M values are approximations ("zona gris de la
tabla") and discourages using them as the reference. The one exception is
RAL 5014 for equipment blue.

Corporate colours per p. 30: Rojo (NCS 0090-Y70R) primary; Gris (NCS 4005-R50B)
secondary, for technical equipment, furniture, trains and station interiors;
Blanco and Negro as neutrals.

Table cells were transcribed by eye from a 1500 px JPEG; small digits in the
CMYK column (e.g. K63 vs K65) may be misread. Check against the attached scan
before using them for print.
